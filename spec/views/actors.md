# Who reviews a specification?

The primary actor is the [specification author or reviewer](../artifacts/actors/ACTOR-001.yaml). They maintain or inspect a structured engineering plan, follow artifact relationships, and compare revisions of a local or gh-accessible repository.

Start with [browsing and link navigation](use-cases.md#read-and-follow-a-plan), [revision comparison](use-cases.md#compare-revisions), or the [browser repository overview](use-cases.md#browser-and-github-review).
